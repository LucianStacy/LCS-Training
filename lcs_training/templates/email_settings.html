What gets sent

Edit the wording, timing and automatic sending on Templates. By default the app drafts:

  • Invoice coming due: 14 days before each installment
  • Invoice past due: 30 days after an invoiced installment's due date
  • Renewal reminder: 60 days before a contract ends
  • Training / audit reminder: 3 days before a scheduled session
  • Welcome: when a fully executed contract is received

Quotes, consult plans and general messages are written from their own pages. Contacts marked no reminder emails are skipped.
